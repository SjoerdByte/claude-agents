# Onpay

Easily integrate Anyday with your Onpay payment solution to provide flexible installment payments to your customers. Here’s how to activate Anyday in your Onpay Manager:

1. **Access Onpay Manager:** Follow the link to Onpay Login and sign in.
2. **Navigate to Settings:** Choose **Indstillinger** from the menu.
3. **Select Providers:** Click on **Udbydere** to view available payment providers.
4. **Locate Anyday:** Find and select **Anyday** from the list.
5. **Account Login:** Click on **Log in** and enter the password associated with your MyAnyday account.
6. **Activate Anyday:** Ensure the activation button turns green by clicking **Aktivere Anyday**, then save your changes by clicking **Gem**.

![](https://files.readme.io/3d8e1e7-unnamed.png)

By completing these steps, Anyday will be activated on your Onpay platform, allowing you to offer a more accessible payment option to your customers.
