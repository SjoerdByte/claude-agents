# Magento 1.x

Enhance your Magento 1 store by integrating Anyday, offering your customers the flexibility to split their payments into manageable installments. To leverage Anyday's payment solution, it's essential to have the Quickpay module installed and configured as the foundation for this integration. Whether you're new to Quickpay or already have the module in place, follow our guides to ensure a smooth setup process, allowing you to offer Anyday's payment options to your customers efficiently.
