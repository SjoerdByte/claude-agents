# Integration Overview

Integrating Anyday into your MCB.Cloud platform is straightforward. Unlike other platforms that require manual setup, MCB.Cloud offers a hands-off integration process for merchants. Simply submit your Anyday API credentials to MCB, and they will manage the entire onboarding process on your behalf.

### Key Integration Highlights:

* **Direct Setup by MCB:** MCB.Cloud handles the integration, minimizing setup efforts for merchants.
* **Seamless Customer Experience:** Offer Anyday's split payment solutions quickly and efficiently on your MCB.Cloud store.
* **Support and Guidance:** MCB provides support throughout the integration process, ensuring a hassle-free setup.

***

*If you do not have a Quickpay account, please contact<onboarding@anyday.io> and we will be happy to create a Quickpay account for you at no cost.*
