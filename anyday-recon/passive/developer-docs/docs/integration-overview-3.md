# Integration Overview

For merchants using Dandomain (old), integrating Anyday as a payment solution involves configuring OnPay as the payment gateway. This overview provides a snapshot of the steps needed to prepare your store for offering Anyday's split payment solution.

### Key Steps for Integration:

1. **Select OnPay as Your Payment Gateway:** Essential for linking Anyday with your Dandomain store.
2. **Configure Payment Method Details:** Customize your payment options to include Anyday.
3. **Activate and Go Live:** Ensure your settings are correctly configured to start offering Anyday payments.

***

*If you do not have a OnPay account, please contact<onboarding@anyday.io> and we will be happy to create a OnPay account for you at no cost.*
