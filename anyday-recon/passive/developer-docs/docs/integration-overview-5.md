# Integration Overview

Integrating Anyday into your Shoporama store involves configuring Quickpay as a payment gateway to facilitate Anyday transactions. This overview simplifies the process, ensuring you can quickly offer Anyday's flexible payment solutions to your customers.

### Key Steps for Integration:

1. **Set Up Quickpay as the Gateway:** Quickpay acts as the conduit for Anyday payments.
2. **Configure the Payment Method:** Customize your Shoporama settings to include Anyday as a distinct option.
3. **Activate and Test:** Finalize the setup to start offering Anyday and conduct tests to ensure smooth operation.

***

*If you do not have a Quickpay account, please contact<onboarding@anyday.io> and we will be happy to create a Quickpay account for you at no cost.*
