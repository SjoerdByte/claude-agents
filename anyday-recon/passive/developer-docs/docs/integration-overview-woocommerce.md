# Integration Overview

## Integration Overview:

Integrating Anyday into your WooCommerce platform transforms the way you handle transactions, providing a seamless split payment option for your customers. This overview will guide you through the benefits and initial steps required for integration, laying the groundwork for a successful setup.

## Benefits of Integrating Anyday:

* **Boost Sales:** Increase your average order value and conversion rates by offering flexible payment options.
* **Improve Customer Satisfaction:** Give your customers the freedom to manage their budgets without delaying their purchase decisions.
* **Quick and Easy Setup:** Follow our straightforward guide to integrate Anyday without any hassle.
* **Secure and Reliable:** Anyday ensures secure transactions, providing peace of mind for both you and your customers.

## Integration Snapshot:

1. **Preliminary Requirements:** Ensure your store runs on WordPress 5.51+, WooCommerce 4.5.2+, PHP 7.2.5+, with an active SSL certificate on checkout pages.
2. **Install Quickpay Plugin:** Access detailed instructions on downloading and activating the Anyday plugin for WooCommerce.
3. **Configure Payment Gateway:** Connect Anyday with your preferred payment gateway, such as Quickpay, ensuring a smooth payment process.

***

*If you do not have a Quickpay account, please contact<onboarding@anyday.io> and we will be happy to create a Quickpay account for you at no cost.*
