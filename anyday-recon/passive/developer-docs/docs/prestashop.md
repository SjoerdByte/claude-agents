# PrestaShop

Discover the power of integrating Anyday with your PrestaShop store to offer a versatile and customer-friendly split payment solution. Anyday allows your customers to split their purchases into convenient monthly installments, directly increasing accessibility to your products without sacrificing immediate revenue. This integration is designed for ease, ensuring you can quickly offer flexible payment options, improve customer satisfaction, and see higher conversion rates.
