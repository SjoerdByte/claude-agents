# Setup Guides (Deprecated)

## Quickpay - Activate your Anyday API Key

### Quickpay Manager

1. Login to your [Quickpay manager](https://manage.quickpay.net/).
2. Go to Settings
3. Click Acquirers
4. Find Anyday on the list
5. Insert your Anyday API key
6. Insert your Anyday Private key
7. Click **Save changes** button.
8. Toggle '**Accept payments with Anyday**' **ON**

## Install the Shopify APP:

If you wish to setup Anyday as an individual payment method, which will allow your customers to choose Anyday before entering the payment window, this is how to do it.

![](https://files.readme.io/1ab4d07-shopify-anyday.png "shopify-anyday.png")

**1.** Log in to <https://manage.quickpay.net/>.

**2.** Go to **Settings** > **External integrations** and click on "Shopify".

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/c6158ba-image.png" />

**3.** Click on **Install app** to the right of the 'Anyday' option. You will now be redirected to your Shopify account where, after logging in, you will be presented with our app which you should click **Install app** on.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/e8d86a7-image.png" />

**4.** You will then be redirected back to your Quickpay account where you need to log in to your user and choose which account the app should be installed on.

**5.** Congratulations. You have now installed the Anyday payment app in Shopify.

## Price Widget

The Price Widget works by adding a script to the page and an HTML element where you'd like to render the price widget. [Follow our guide for implementing the Price Widget on your Webshop](https://developer.anyday.io/docs/price-widget-script)

## Optional Installation Service

Should you prefer a hassle-free setup, our team is ready to handle the installation process for you. To enable this service, we require collaboration access to your Shopify account. You can grant this access by navigating to your Shopify dashboard, under **Settings** > **Users and Permissions**.

Please locate the collaboration access code in the '**Users and Permissions**' section and email it to us at <onboarding@anyday.io.> Upon receipt of your code, we will initiate a collaboration request from our Shopify Partner account.

Once you approve our request, our expert team will begin the installation of the Anyday payment app on your Shopify store. We aim to complete all setups within 48 hours.
