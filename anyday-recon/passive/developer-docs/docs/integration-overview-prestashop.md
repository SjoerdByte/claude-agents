# Integration Overview

Integrating Anyday with your PrestaShop platform enriches your e-commerce operation by providing a split payment option that benefits both you and your customers. This section outlines the benefits of integration and the preliminary steps to get started, ensuring a seamless transition to a more flexible payment system.

## Why Integrate Anyday?

* **Boost Sales and Conversions:** Make your products more accessible and reduce cart abandonment by offering split payments.
* **Enhance Customer Experience:** Allow customers the flexibility to manage their finances without compromising on their purchase decisions.
* **Simple Integration Process:** Follow our straightforward guide to integrate Anyday, enhancing your store's functionality with minimal effort.
* **Secure and Supported:** Rely on a secure payment solution, supported by Anyday's comprehensive customer service.

## Initial Integration Steps:

1. **Check Requirements:** Ensure your PrestaShop installation is compatible with Anyday's split payment solution.
2. **App Installation:** Understand how to download and install the Anyday module for PrestaShop, setting the stage for configuration.
