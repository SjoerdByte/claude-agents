# Getting Started with Anyday

This page will help you get started with Anyday. You'll be up and running in a jiffy!

## Welcome to Anyday's Developer Documentation

Thank you for choosing Anyday! Our documentation is designed to guide you through integrating and maximizing the Anyday payment solution for your e-commerce platform. To get started, let's clarify some key terms you'll encounter throughout this guide.

***

## Glossary of Terms

* **Merchant:** The business entity offering products or services through an online store, utilizing Anyday as their payment solution. Each merchant is associated with a unique login account on Anyday.
* **Webshop:** The online storefront operated by a merchant to showcase and sell products. A merchant may manage one or multiple webshops under their Anyday account.
* **Debtor:** The customer visiting the webshop with the intention to purchase products. Debtors use Anyday to finance their purchases, allowing them to pay over time.
* **Price Widget:** A dynamic tool provided by Anyday, displayed on product pages to show the monthly installment price when opting for Anyday's payment method.
