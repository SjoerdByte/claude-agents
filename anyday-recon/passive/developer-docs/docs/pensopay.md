# Pensopay

If you're using Pensopay and decide to add Anyday as a payment option, rest assured that there's no need for you to manually activate Anyday within Pensopay. We coordinate directly with Pensopay to ensure Anyday is activated on your behalf.

**Key Point:**

* While specific setup steps may vary based on your shop system, the activation of Anyday through Pensopay is handled internally. This means one less step for you, allowing you to focus on your business, not on payment gateway configurations.

Stay informed on any shop system-specific setup requirements to fully integrate Anyday, but know that the initial activation with Pensopay is seamless and straightforward.
