# Billwerk+ (Reepay)

Integrate Anyday with your Billwerk+ platform to offer flexible payment options to your customers. Follow these simple steps to activate Anyday in your Billwerk+ (Reepay) manager:

1. **Log in** to your account at [Billwerk+ Login](https://app.reepay.com/).
2. Navigate to **Configuration/Konfiguration**.
3. Select Payment **Methods/Betalingsmetoder**.
4. Locate **Anyday** and click on it.
5. **Insert the Anyday API key** provided to you.
6. **Enter the provided Private key**.
7. **Activate Anyday** by clicking on **Create/Opret**.

By following these steps, Anyday will be activated and ready to use within your Billwerk+ platform, allowing you to offer a more flexible payment solution to your customers.
