# Setup Guides

For Dandomain (old) users, this guide walks you through the process of setting up Anyday using OnPay as your payment gateway.

### Activating Anyday with OnPay:

1. **Access Your Dandomain Backend:** Go to **Backend > Indstillinger > Betaling**.
2. **Add a New Payment Method:** Click on **+Tilføj** and fill in the details:
   * **Payment Method Name:** Enter "Anyday".
   * **Payment Method Note:** Enter "Del din betaling op med Anyday".
3. **Upload and Link Your Logo:** Upload the Anyday logo to your gallery from [Anyday's Marketing Toolkit](https://da.anyday.io/marketing-toolkit) and insert the URL for **Payment Method Icon** and **Payment Method Image**.
4. **Select OnPay Payment Window V3:** Under **Vælg betalingsløsning**, choose **OnPay betalingsvindue V3**.
5. **Enter Your OnPay Details:** Input your **Gateway ID** and **Security Key** from OnPay.
6. **Set Status to Live:** Ensure the payment method status is set to **Live**.
7. **Choose Anyday as the Payment Method:** From the payment method options, select **Anyday**.
8. **Assign Denmark as the Delivery Country:** Link Denmark as the chosen delivery country.
9. **Save Your Configuration:** Click **Gem** to apply your settings.

By completing these steps, your Dandomain (old) store will be ready to offer Anyday's flexible payment options, enhancing the shopping experience for your customers.
