# Integration Overview

Integrating Anyday into your Magento 1 store involves setting up the Quickpay payment module as a prerequisite. This overview provides a snapshot of the steps needed to prepare your store for Anyday's split payment solution, ensuring you meet all requirements for a successful integration.

### Key Steps for Integration:

1. **Install Quickpay Module:** Essential for linking Anyday with your Magento 1 store.
2. **Configure Payment Methods:** Adjust settings within Magento to activate the Quickpay module.
3. **Enter API Details:** Required for seamless communication between Magento, Quickpay, and Anyday.
4. **Adjust Order Status Settings:** To align with your store's order management process.

Follow these foundational steps to prepare your Magento 1 store for the Anyday integration.

***

*If you do not have a Quickpay account, please contact<onboarding@anyday.io> and we will be happy to create a Quickpay account for you at no cost.*
