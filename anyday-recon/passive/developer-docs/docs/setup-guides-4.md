# Setup Guides

This guide details the steps to establish Anyday as a standalone payment method in your Shoporama checkout process, leveraging Quickpay for transaction management.

### Activating Anyday with Quickpay:

1. **Navigate to Settings:** Click the settings icon in the top right corner of your Shoporama backend, then select **gateways.**
2. **Create a New Payment Gateway:** Opt for **"Create new payment gateway"** and set the type to **"QuickPay v10"**.
3. **Input Required Data:** Obtain the necessary integration details from your [Quickpay manager](https://manage.quickpay.net/) (found under Settings > Integration) and fill them into your Shoporama setup.
4. **Rename the Integration:** Change the integration name to Anyday to clearly identify the payment method.
5. **Specify Payment Methods:** In the **"Betalingsmetoder"** field, enter **"anyday-split"** to designate the Anyday payment option.
6. **Save Your Configuration:** Click **"Gem"** to apply and save your settings.

By following these instructions, you'll successfully add Anyday as an individual payment method within your Shoporama store, offering a transparent and flexible payment choice to your customers.
