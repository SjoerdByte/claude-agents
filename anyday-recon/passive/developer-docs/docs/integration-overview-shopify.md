# Integration Overview (Deprecated)

### Integration Overview:

Leveraging Anyday with your Shopify store opens up new avenues for your business, providing a split payment option that’s easy for both you and your customers to use. This overview highlights the key benefits and the initial steps for integrating Anyday, ensuring you’re well-prepared to enhance your e-commerce strategy.

### Key Benefits:

* **Elevate Customer Experience:** Offer your customers the flexibility to pay over time, making purchases more accessible.
* **Increase Conversion Rates:** Reduce cart abandonment by providing a convenient and transparent payment solution.
* **Fast and Simple Integration:** Get Anyday up and running on your Shopify store with ease, thanks to our straightforward setup process.
* **Safe and Secure:** Trust in a payment solution that’s secure for you and your customers, backed by comprehensive support.

### Getting Started:

1. **Compatibility Check:** Confirm that your store is ready for Anyday by ensuring it meets our basic requirements.
2. **App Installation:** Learn how to find and install the Anyday app for Shopify, either directly through the Shopify App Store or via your payment gateway provider.
