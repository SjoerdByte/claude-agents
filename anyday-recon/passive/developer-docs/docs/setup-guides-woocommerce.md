# Setup Guides

## Implementation

Our comprehensive setup guides are designed to assist you through every step of integrating Anyday with your WooCommerce store. From installation to configuration, we've got you covered.

## Step-by-Step Installation and Configuration:

1. **Install the Anyday Plugin:**
   * Navigate to your WordPress dashboard.
   * Go to "Plugins" > "Add New" and search for the Quickpay plugin.
   * Install and activate the plugin.

2. **Configure Anyday Settings:**
   * Within your WordPress dashboard, navigate to "WooCommerce" > "Settings" > "Payments."
   * Locate Quickpay in the payment methods list and click "Manage."
   * Enter your Quickpay API key and Private key (obtain these from your Quickpay manager).

3. **Testing and Going Live:**
   * Perform test transactions to ensure everything is set up correctly.
   * Once confirmed, enable Anyday payments to go live on your store.

## Need Assistance?

If you encounter any issues or have questions during the setup process, refer to our detailed documentation at Anyday Developer Docs or contact our support team for personalized assistance.
