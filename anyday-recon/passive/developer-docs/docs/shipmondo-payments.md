# Shipmondo Payments

Integrating Anyday for merchants using Shipmondo Payments is designed to be hassle-free. When you choose Anyday as a payment option, there's no manual activation required on your part within Shipmondo Payments.

**Key Highlight:**

* Shipmondo Payments automatically activates Anyday for you, following coordination between Anyday and Shipmondo. Although setup steps might differ depending on your specific e-commerce platform, the activation of Anyday is directly managed, simplifying the process for you.

Ensure you're prepared for any additional setup actions that might be needed for your particular shop system to fully utilize Anyday, but rest easy knowing the activation process through Shipmondo Payments is taken care of.
