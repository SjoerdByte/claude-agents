## Endpoints (merchant-facing, verbatim from ANYDAY-WooCommerce plugin & ANYDAY-Magento2 plugin)
- POST /api/v1/authentication/login             (Magento2 ManagerInterface.php ANYDAY_AUTH_URL)
- POST /api/v1/orders                           (authorize payment)
- POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/capture
- POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/cancel
- POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/refund
- GET  /api/v1/orders?id={transaction}
- Auth header: Authorization: Bearer <token>
- Content-Type: application/json
- Token selection depends on adm_environment (live|test) and adm_authentication_type (auth_manual|auth_account)
- Request body carries callbackUrl pointing at merchant web-hook (potential SSRF / mass-assign surface worth testing)

## Internal (web-facing, unauthenticated jQuery GETs) — my.anyday.io
- Base: https://my.anyday.io/api/{v1|v2}/internal/
- Hit endpoints: shops (v2), categories (v1), categories/{catId} (v1)
- No Authorization header on these calls — public catalog surface
