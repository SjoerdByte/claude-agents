# CSP Breakdown — substack.com family (observed 2026-10-05)

## substack.com (/, /home, /sign-in, /app, /admin)
content-security-policy: frame-ancestors 'self' https://*.substack.com https://substack.com

Notes:
- Only a frame-ancestors directive is set. No connect-src, script-src, img-src,
  default-src, or style-src is declared. This is a very permissive posture —
  the browser falls back to its defaults for everything except framing.
- Framing is allowed only from substack.com and *.substack.com; third-party
  sites cannot iframe substack pages (anti-clickjacking posture is OK).

## api.substack.com
content-security-policy: frame-ancestors 'self' https://*.substack.com https://substack.com
(responds HTTP/2 404 at /, Cloudflare in front, x-powered-by: Express)

## on.substack.com
content-security-policy: frame-ancestors 'self' https://*.substack.com https://substack.com
(Cloudflare in front, x-sub: on, x-service: web, Express)

## Additional non-CSP security-relevant headers (observed on substack.com):
- Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload
- x-powered-by: Express                 (fingerprint — tells attackers the stack)
- x-cluster: substack
- x-deploy: 52a7d37c7f                  (deploy/commit identifier — rotate)
- x-service: web
- x-served-by: Substack
- Set-Cookie: AWSALBTG / AWSALBTGCORS   (AWS Application Load Balancer target-group stickiness)
- Set-Cookie: __cf_bm                   (Cloudflare bot management)
- Set-Cookie: ab_experiment_sampled, ab_testing_id, cookie_storage_key,
             ajs_anonymous_id (Segment anonymous id), visit_id

## Implications for recon
- CSP is NOT constraining what the SPA may call: egress inventory must come
  from JS bundles (done — see grep-aggregated.txt).
- AWS ALB + Cloudflare edge + Express origin confirmed from headers.
- Segment analytics is in use (ajs_anonymous_id cookie).
