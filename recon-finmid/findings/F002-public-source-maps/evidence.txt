### Why this is a real finding

The combined effect of the two public maps is that the attack surface the
rest of this engagement is working against (webhooks IDOR, buyer /exists
oracle, publicSignup state-machine, payables-financing/public/*) was
reconstructable in a few hours entirely from the publicly-served bundles —
there was no need for authenticated enumeration or code leak from any
other source. Independent of the specific downstream exploit chains, that
is the standard definition of an information-disclosure finding
(CWE-540 / CWE-200).
